## Trust signals (ratings, guarantees, refunds, safety claims, booster verification)

### Takeaway
The leading sites put their Trustpilot reputation front and center: 4.4–4.8 scores with thousands to tens of thousands of reviews, and very high reply rates to negative reviews. They pair it with explicit safety claims ("offline mode", "VPN-shielded", "Challenger minimum", "zero bans since 2021") and loosely worded guarantees ("100% satisfaction", "Fair Refunds", "progress-based refunds"). Refund terms in this industry are almost always progress-proportional: full refund before start, partial after, none after completion. Bans remain the dominant 1-star complaint everywhere.

### Cited Findings
**Trustpilot profiles (fetched 2026-11-14)**
- GameBoost: TrustScore 4.5 "Excellent", 24,178 reviews (8,884 in the last 12 months). 88% are 5-star and 8% are 1-star. Replies to 69% of negative reviews, typically within a month, and may use Trustpilot's AI reply assist. Invites customers to review. Claims "2,100,000+ orders" and "3,000+ partners", with an address in Zagreb, Croatia. Complaints focus on reclaimed or banned purchased accounts and seller disputes. Praise focuses on fast support. — [Trustpilot GameBoost](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/gameboost.com)
- Skycoach: 4.4, 28,801 reviews (8,024 in the last 12 months). 90% are 5-star and 7% are 1-star. Replies to 99% of negative reviews, typically within 24h, with templated replies that point to live chat. Complaints: slow order start, automated or inconsistent support, unresolved refund requests, bans. One reviewer alleges compensation was offered in exchange for removing a review (unverified). — [Trustpilot Skycoach](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/skycoach.gg)
- BoostRoyal: 4.7, 9,803 reviews (only 112 in the last 12 months). 93% are 5-star. Replies to 92% of negative reviews within about a week. Trustpilot flags that the company has not recently invited reviews. Complaints: bans after boosts, and compensation (extra games, discount codes) judged inadequate. Company replies say "No method can guarantee 100% protection". — [Trustpilot BoostRoyal](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/boostroyal.com)
- GGBoost: 4.7, 7,040 reviews (930 in the last 12 months). 94% are 5-star and 3% are 1-star. Replies to 100% of negative reviews within 24h. Complaints: temporary suspensions, LP loss, a booster playing unrequested champions, and an allegation of paid reviews (disputed). — [Trustpilot GGBoost](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/ggboost.com)
- Boosting Factory: 4.8, 1,689 reviews (354 in the last 12 months). 94% are 5-star. Replies to 100% of negative reviews within a week. Complaints: delays in assignment, communication and progress visibility. — [Trustpilot BoostingFactory](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/boostingfactory.com)
- Eloking: 4.4, 1,497 reviews (only 30 in the last 12 months). 96% are 5-star. Replies to 80% of negative reviews, typically taking more than a month. Complaints: "Dead support live chat and cannot find booster for 24h", boosters going silent, bans, hard-to-get refunds. Eloking says it refunds bans "with proof". — [Trustpilot Eloking](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/eloking.com)
- eloboost.net ("EB 24"): only 8 reviews. Trustpilot says the company's website has closed, and the last review is from June 2022. This is consistent with eloboost.net returning HTTP 521. — [Trustpilot eloboost.net](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/eloboost.net)

**On-site trust claims**
- Eloking: "100% satisfaction guarantee", a "4.5★ on Trustpilot" badge, "Zero bans since 2021.", "your booster plays in offline mode so you stay invisible to your friends list", "Payments on Eloking are end to end encrypted and safe", "We do not store your payment details". Discloses its company registration (Eloking Ltd., Riga, Latvia, reg. 40203341307). "20,000+ satisfied customers". Boosters are screened "for attitude, not just rank". — [eloking.com](https://eloking.com/)
- BoostRoyal: on-site rating "4.86" from "79.4K customer ratings" (its own review system, separate from Trustpilot). "BoostRoyal Guarantee" promises "verified boosters, shielded sessions and transparent buyer protection". "Every player passes a multi-stage verification", "the minimum requirement to boost is Challenger". "VPN-shielded sessions and 256-bit SSL encryption", "Strict Offline Mode" is "free on every order". "progress-based refunds through a transparent dispute flow", "no hidden fees". "never had an issue with account theft". "Verified" tags on reviews. Riot non-affiliation disclaimer. — [boostroyal.com](https://www.boostroyal.com/)
- Boosteria: "Fast, Safe, Guaranteed!", "Fair Refunds", "0.1% Elite Boosters", "Trusted by 100,000+ Gamers". No Trustpilot widget or VPN/offline claims on the homepage. — [boosteria.org](https://boosteria.org/)
- Skycoach: big-number stats ("1.2 million completed orders", "200 thousand happy customers", "3 thousand In-house PROs", "5 years on the market"). A "Legit" page in the footer. Company disclosure: DEVAL LIMITED, Nicosia, Cyprus. No money-back guarantee stated on the homepage. — [skycoach.gg](https://skycoach.gg/)

**Refund term patterns (third-party and smaller sites; the major sites' policy pages could not be loaded)**
- EloHeaven (mirror, authenticity unverified): the team sets partial refund amounts based on work done and the difficulty of the remaining work, and all sales are final once a booster begins. — [eloheaven refund policy](https://eloheaven-frontend.pages.dev/resources/refund-policy)
- Eloboss: completed orders are final, and a chargeback on a partly performed order is treated as a breach of terms. — [eloboss.net/refund-policy](https://eloboss.net/refund-policy)
- Grindout: refunds only if the order was not started or queued. — [grindout.com/refund-policy](https://grindout.com/refund-policy)
- Immortal Boost Q&A (2023): 100% refund if the order isn't processed, 50% if paused after processing starts, partial if nearly finished. — [smartcustomer.com](https://www.smartcustomer.com/faq/immortalboost.com/what-is-the-refund-policy-please-as-i-want-to-place-the-order-but-before-that-i-need-to-know-about-your-refund-policy-in-case)
- Independent review sites note that GameBoost account-resale buyers report reclaimed logins and refunds refused "once a short warranty lapses" (affiliate-style source, low reliability). — [safeboosting.net](https://safeboosting.net/is-gameboost-legit/)

### Inferences
- A visible Trustpilot score with a review count is table stakes. Leaders also reply to almost every negative review within 24h–1 week, which is itself a trust signal shown on Trustpilot.
- Review recency matters. Eloking (30 reviews/yr) and BoostRoyal (112/yr) have stale profiles, while GameBoost and Skycoach collect about 8k/yr, likely through automated post-order review invitations.
- A site without public reviews lacks the single most-used trust device in this niche. An on-site review system with "Verified" tags plus Trustpilot invites, as BoostRoyal does, closes this gap.
- Explicit "offline mode", "VPN" and booster-tier claims are standard copy for LoL/Valorant. Ban complaints persist regardless, so a clear, quotable ban/refund policy is a differentiator.

### Gaps
- The exact refund-policy wording of GameBoost, Eloking, Skycoach, BoostRoyal and Boosting Factory could not be retrieved (403, 404 or JS-only pages).
- No data on Google reviews ratings for these sites.
- Ban rates and the "zero bans" claims are unverifiable.

## Support channels

### Takeaway
Leaders advertise 24/7 live chat as the primary channel, often with phone, a mobile app, or Discord as well. Tickets alone are below the market norm. Formal response-time SLAs are rare; sites instead make operational promises ("booster contact in under 30 minutes").

### Cited Findings
- BoostRoyal: "Dedicated 24/7 Support", "Live chat, phone and ticket coverage around the clock". Mobile apps on the App Store and Google Play. Order-completion alerts and dashboard status. No Discord shown. — [boostroyal.com](https://www.boostroyal.com/)
- Eloking: "Live support", "Ping us in live chat or through the contact page", a Discord server link, booster-chat in the Members Area. "Region confirmation in minutes" and "booster contact in under 30 minutes". — [eloking.com](https://eloking.com/). Trustpilot reviewers complain that live chat is sometimes dead. — [Trustpilot Eloking](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/eloking.com)
- Skycoach: "Chat with us", phone +1 (855) 401 11 56, email, and a mobile app to "chat with a PRO on your phone". Its Trustpilot replies route customers to live chat. — [skycoach.gg](https://skycoach.gg/); [Trustpilot Skycoach](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/skycoach.gg)
- Boosteria: only "Professional Support!" plus Contacts and FAQ pages. No visible live chat. — [boosteria.org](https://boosteria.org/)
- GameBoost: Trustpilot praise centers on support resolving issues "within minutes". — [Trustpilot GameBoost](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/gameboost.com)

### Inferences
- Live chat (and ideally direct customer–booster chat on the order) is expected. Email or push notifications on order events and a mobile app are differentiators used by the top two volume players.

### Gaps
- Which chat vendor (Intercom, Crisp, Tawk) each site uses could not be determined, because the fetched text does not expose widget scripts.
- Discord member counts were not retrieved.

## Payments (methods, currencies, VAT)

### Takeaway
The top sites offer 10–15+ payment methods: PayPal, Apple Pay and Google Pay, crypto, paysafecard, Skrill/Neteller, iDEAL/EPS/P24/Bancontact, and BNPL (Klarna, Sezzle). Skycoach adds a currency selector (EUR/USD) and four languages. Card-only, single-currency checkout is well below the competitive set.

### Cited Findings
- Skycoach: PayPal, Visa/Mastercard, Apple Pay, Google Pay, EPS, iDEAL, Skrill, Neteller, Przelewy24, Rapid Transfer, Bitcoin and "BTC, ETH, USDT, USDC and more", Klarna, Sezzle ("Pay now only 25% of the amount and split the remaining into several payments"). EUR currency selector in the header. Languages: EN/FR/ES/DE. — [skycoach.gg](https://skycoach.gg/)
- BoostRoyal: Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover, Google Pay, Apple Pay, Amazon Pay, Bitcoin, PayPal, iDEAL, Revolut, Bancontact, EPS, paysafecard. No visible currency selector (prices appear in USD). — [boostroyal.com](https://www.boostroyal.com/)
- Eloking: shows an unlabeled "Accepted Payment Methods" image, "secure payment, instant confirmation", and a nine-language selector. — [eloking.com](https://eloking.com/)
- Boosteria: eight-language selector (EN, ES, DE, FR, AR, NL, PT, ZH). Payment methods not shown on the homepage. — [boosteria.org](https://boosteria.org/)

### Inferences
- The minimum competitive set appears to be cards, PayPal, Apple/Google Pay and crypto, plus EU local methods and paysafecard for a younger, card-less audience. BNPL is used for larger orders.

### Gaps
- VAT handling (inclusive or exclusive pricing, OSS registration) was not visible on any fetched page.
- GameBoost and Boosting Factory payment lists were not retrieved (403).

## Promotions and retention (coupons, cashback, loyalty, referral/affiliate)

### Takeaway
Wallet cashback with spend-based tiers is the dominant retention mechanic (BoostRoyal up to 8%, Eloking 3/5/7%). Other common devices: referral rewards (Skycoach "Share your link, get $20"), affiliate programs at about 5–10% revenue share, aggressive seasonal coupon codes (Boosteria "Save 51% with Promo Code: PreBlack51"), gamified daily rewards (Eloking lootbox), and membership tiers ("Boosteria Premium", "Loyalty").

### Cited Findings
- BoostRoyal: "Every order earns cashback in your wallet — up to 8%, based on your last 12 months. Spend it on any service." Tiers: Bronze 3%, Gold 5%, Diamond 6%, Challenger 8%. Also sells vouchers and gift cards and has a newsletter signup. — [boostroyal.com](https://www.boostroyal.com/)
- Eloking: "Cashback on all Eloking boosts (3%, 5%, 7%)", "Discounts for all Eloking boosts (5%, 10%, 15%)", "Discount for a friend", "Free daily lootbox spin with industry-leading rewards", "Member exclusive season and event offers", and free LoL/CS boost pages. — [eloking.com](https://eloking.com/)
- Skycoach: "Share your link, get $20!" (/cashback referral), an affiliate program ("Earn by recommending Skycoach to your audience"), and "Hot right now" and "Most-wanted offers" sections. — [skycoach.gg](https://skycoach.gg/)
- Boosteria: "Pre-Black Sale! Cheap Ranks! Save 51% with Promo Code: PreBlack51", a scarcity counter "Offers for Today Left: 5", and /loyalty and /premium pages. — [boosteria.org](https://boosteria.org/)
- GameBoost affiliate: up to 6.30% per sale with a 60-day validation (aggregator listing). — [CueLinks](https://www.cuelinks.com/campaigns/gameboost-affiliate-program). A purported terms document lists 5–10% by category (10% for accounts and items, 5% for currency and keys), with payment monthly after a 15-day lock (authenticity unverified). — [Scribd](https://www.scribd.com/document/963274284/GameBoost-TemplateTerms)
- Eloboss affiliate: "7% (or more) lifetime revenue share" on all orders from referred customers, credited to the account balance. — [eloboss.net/affiliate-program](https://eloboss.net/affiliate-program)
- Third-party review sites distribute coupon codes (e.g., AskBoosters' "askboosters" code for GameBoost), showing that coupon-code affiliates are an acquisition channel. — [askboosters.com](https://askboosters.com/gameboost/)
- BoostRoyal uses discount codes and free games as ban/complaint compensation. — [Trustpilot BoostRoyal](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/boostroyal.com)

### Inferences
- The minimum to compete: a coupon engine (also needed for ban/complaint compensation and affiliate codes), wallet balance with tiered cashback, and a referral link. Affiliate rates of about 5–10% lifetime are the reference point.

### Gaps
- GameBoost, Skycoach and Boosting Factory cashback or loyalty specifics were not retrieved.
- Email-marketing practices (cadence, abandoned-cart emails) could not be observed beyond newsletter signups.

## Conversion UX

### Takeaway
Rank-to-rank calculators with live pricing, add-ons (role/champion, streaming, duo, offline mode), live order tracking, big social-proof numbers, scarcity banners, multi-language selectors and mobile apps are standard. Account creation is usually folded into or after checkout rather than required upfront.

### Cited Findings
- BoostRoyal: current/desired rank pickers, a calculator with exact pricing, role and champion requests, a Streaming add-on, a duo vs solo choice, "most orders are completed within 1-2 days", "Secure checkout · Instant assignment", a live tracker showing "Victory · +24 LP", a "Compare LoL boosting sellers" page, and booster cards showing "3,667 orders done". — [boostroyal.com](https://www.boostroyal.com/)
- Eloking: a five-step process explainer, a "Password creation prompt after checkout" (an account is created after payment, which is effectively guest-style checkout), live tracking with an optional stream link, and nine languages. — [eloking.com](https://eloking.com/)
- Skycoach: testimonials with relative dates, offer counts per game, "Hot right now" sections, installment options, and app promotion. — [skycoach.gg](https://skycoach.gg/)
- Boosteria: Google sign-in, a "Free Sign Up", a countdown/scarcity counter, and per-game "Demo" pages. — [boosteria.org](https://boosteria.org/)

### Inferences
- Account creation after payment, social login, live progress with stream links, and recency-stamped reviews are common, low-cost wins.

### Gaps
- No site showed a "recent orders" live ticker in the fetched text. Sticky checkout behavior could not be confirmed without rendering the pages.
